Infinite zoom

Shallow views are computed directly on the GPU. Deeper views use perturbation: one orbit is computed with exact-precision arithmetic, and every pixel follows it as a small offset on the GPU. Every depth renders at full display resolution. With Auto, the iteration limit grows as you zoom so deep structure stays resolved.

What the image can tell you

Fixed points and period-2 orbits are candidates observed over a finite number of iterations. Crossing |w| > 10¹⁰ does not prove divergence. Unresolved pixels may include slower or longer-period behavior. Different precision modes can disagree near boundaries and inside chaotic regions.

Image and motion

Ultra renders at display resolution, up to 2× pixel density and 8.3 million pixels, then adds 4 and 16 orbit samples where edges are detected. Moving shows the current image instantly and refines it while you explore; it does not invent detail. Color flow slowly rotates the palette without recalculating the fractal. It starts only when you turn it on and pauses in the background.

Limits

Minimum span: 1e−200. Reference precision: up to 256 decimal places. Iterations: up to 16,384. The camera stores 256 decimal places; arithmetic is not interval-certified. Very deep views with high iteration limits can take seconds to refine.

Computation stays in your browser. No account, external computation API or app analytics. Saved views use local storage only when you save them.
