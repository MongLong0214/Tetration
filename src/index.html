What the image can tell you

Fixed points and period-2 orbits are candidates observed over a finite number of iterations. Crossing |w| > 10¹⁰ does not prove divergence. Unresolved pixels may include slower or longer-period behavior. Different precision modes can disagree near boundaries.

Limits

Minimum span: 1e−200. Orbit precision: up to 240 decimal places. Deep views: at most 72 horizontal samples. Deep computation can be slow. The camera stores 256 decimal places; the arithmetic is not interval-certified. Infinite precision, unlimited resolution and real-time rendering are not guaranteed.

Computation stays in your browser. No account, external computation API or app analytics. Saved views use local storage only when you save them.
